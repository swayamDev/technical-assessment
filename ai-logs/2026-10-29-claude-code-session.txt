  Realtime tenant portal

  When the gateway settles a bill, the tenant's open portal shows "Payment
  received" and updates itself with no reload.

  My first approach leaked, and the tests caught it. I started with Supabase's
  Postgres Changes feature. The isolation tests showed that an anonymous user
  received a notification every time a payment or bill changed. The events
  carried no data (empty rows marked "401 Unauthorized"), but anyone with the
  public key could see payment activity as it happened.

  I replaced it with private Broadcast channels:
  - When a payment or bill changes, the database sends a data-free "changed"
    signal on a private channel for that lease.
  - Database security rules let only that lease's own tenant join that channel,
    and nobody can send on it.
  - The portal then re-reads its data through the normal row-level security.

  Tested (20 e2e checks plus a browser run):
  - The owning tenant receives the signals, and they carry no row data.
  - Other tenants, the other organization's users, same-organization staff and
    anonymous users cannot join the channel, even knowing the lease id.
  - Joining the same topic as a public channel receives nothing.
  - Forged broadcasts are not delivered.
  - In the browser, Ben's portal went from RM 3,501.02 due to RM 0.00 by itself.
